How to Use:

  • Add Sticks: Ctrl+Click or long-press on mobile
  • Move Sticks: Drag from the middle
  • Resize/Rotate: Drag from the ends
  • Select: Click on a stick to select it
  • Delete: Click the X button on a stick
  • Toggle Grid: Press 'G' key
  • Toggle Sampling Points: Press 'S' key
